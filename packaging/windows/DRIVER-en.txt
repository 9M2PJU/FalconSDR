FalconSDR - RTL-SDR dongle driver (Windows)
===========================================

Windows only talks to an RTL-SDR dongle through the WinUSB driver. It is
installed once, with the free Zadig tool.

If SDR++, SDR#, SDR Console or another SDR program already works with your
dongle, the driver is already installed - nothing to do.

Installing the driver (about 2 minutes):

  1. Plug the dongle into USB. If Windows offers its own driver
     (e.g. "TV tuner"), don't install it.
  2. Download Zadig: https://zadig.akeo.ie/  and run it
     (allow the administrator prompt).
  3. In the menu choose Options -> List All Devices.
  4. Pick "Bulk-In, Interface (Interface 0)" or "RTL2838UHIDIR" from the list.
     Do NOT pick "Interface 1", or your keyboard, mouse or headset.
  5. The driver to the right of the arrow must be "WinUSB". Click
     "Replace Driver" (or "Install Driver") and wait until it finishes.
  6. Unplug the dongle, plug it back in and press Start in FalconSDR.

Note: plugging the dongle into a different USB port may need these steps
again.

The same instructions are in the app: "Listen" page -> "SDR device" card ->
"Dongle not detected? Driver (Zadig) instructions".

The FalconSDR interface runs in your browser: http://127.0.0.1:8765
The program runs in the background - quit it in Settings -> Advanced -> Quit.
